 🎯 Overview

TalentTie is a centralized digital platform connecting Ethiopian models with recruiters, agencies, brands, and production companies. We're solving the fragmentation in Ethiopia's modeling industry by creating a nationwide talent marketplace.

🚨 The Problem

- 130 million people in Ethiopia with 70% youth, yet the modeling industry is completely fragmented
- Models rely solely on social media, referrals, and personal connections
- Only models in Addis Ababa have access to agents and opportunities
- No centralized hub to connect talent with opportunities
- International agencies interested in Ethiopian talent have no efficient way to discover local models

 ✅ The Solution

TalentTie is a digital platform where:

- Models create verified profiles with portfolio, stats, and professional photos
- Recruiters (agencies, brands, TV stations, social media companies) browse talent and contact models directly
- Opportunities available nationwide not limited to Addis Ababa
- Verified profiles ensure trust and professionalism
- Secure messaging between models and recruiters

 🌟 Key Features

 For Models:
- Profile creation with portfolio showcase
- Stats & availability (height, experience, specialization)
- Direct messaging with recruiters
- Booking management
- Safety & verification tools

 For Recruiters:
- Advanced talent search & filtering
- Browse verified model profiles
- Direct contact & booking
- Track applications & hires
- Portfolio reviews

 Platform Features:
- Nationwide coverage (not just Addis Ababa)
- Multiple modeling categories:
  - International modeling
  - Local TV commercials
  - Social media modeling
  - Fashion & runway
- Verified profiles & identity checks
- Dispute resolution & trust features

 💰 Revenue Model

1. Recruiter Subscriptions (Primary)
   - Monthly/annual plans for agencies & brands
   - Advanced search & featured listings

2. Commission on Bookings
   - 10-15% commission on contracted jobs

3. Premium Model Profiles
   - Featured profile boosts
   - Profile analytics & insights

4. Featured Listings
   - Sponsored opportunities
   - Brand partnerships

 🎯 Market Opportunity

- Target Market Size: 91M youth in Ethiopia
- Initial Focus: Addis Ababa + 2 major cities
- Growth Runway: International agencies + local brands
- Zero Competition: No existing platform in Ethiopia
